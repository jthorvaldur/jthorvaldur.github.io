About FarmerDrop

What is FarmerDrop?

FarmerDrop connects members with local farms through neighborhood pickup sites called HostDrops. Members order from farmers, who label and deliver their packages. The host records what arrives and holds the packages for pickup.

Our Mission

Our goal is a resilient, decentralized food network that connects farms with pickup sites run by their neighbors.

Our Values

Support sustainable farming and make it clear who grew your food and where you can pick it up.

Why We Exist

We want fresh, affordable food to be easier to reach. A shared pickup point lets farmers group their deliveries and lets members collect food from several farms in one trip.
